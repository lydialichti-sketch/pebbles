# Pebbles

A calm little to-do app for big goals, built around one idea: you don't do a mountain, you climb it.

- **Mountains** are big goals.
- **Boulders** are the main tasks on the way up.
- **Pebbles** are tiny steps you can finish in one sitting.

Three screens:

- **Dump**: empty your head into the pile.
- **Shape**: sort the pile into mountains, boulders and pebbles.
- **Do**: one pebble at a time, or a timed run.

It's a single `index.html` with no build step. Open it in a browser, or host it anywhere static (GitHub Pages works).

Outside Claude, your data is saved in your browser on that device.

## Use it like an app

Open the GitHub Pages link in Safari on your iPhone, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Each person who opens the link starts with their own empty Pebbles.
